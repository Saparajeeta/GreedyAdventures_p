# About Greedy Adventures
Good people. Great adventures.
One jump changed everything.
The feeling stayed. The adventures grew.
Greedy Adventures began with a tandem jump and a simple idea: make it easier for more people to experience that feeling for themselves.
Today we help first-timers with tandem skydiving in Thailand and India. Paragliding, scuba diving and snowboarding are coming soon.

## Our approach to adventure
Room for your first time.
A little nervous? That is a perfectly good place to start. Explore beginner experiences and ask the crew about the briefing, equipment and requirements before you go.

People make the adventure.
A good day begins before the jump. We connect you with the dropzone and help you understand the plan, so you arrive with your questions answered.

Your story starts with a hello.
Tell us what you are curious about. We will help you find a place to begin.
