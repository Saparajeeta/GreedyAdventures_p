## Before you message
### Do I need any experience to do a tandem jump?
None at all. On a tandem you are harnessed to a USPA-certified instructor who handles the exit, the freefall and the landing. You get a full briefing on the ground first — around 20 minutes — and that is all the preparation you need.

### What are the age and weight limits?
You must be 18 or older with valid ID. The weight limit is 100 kg for tandem jumps, and we ask that your weight is proportional to your height for harness fit. If you are close to the limit, message us before booking and we will confirm.

### Do I have to pay in advance?
No. Send us your preferred date on WhatsApp and we will hold your slot. You pay at the dropzone on the day of the jump.

### What happens if the weather is bad?
Jumps run on wind and cloud conditions, not the clock. If a load is held, you wait for the next window the same day, or we move you to another date at no cost. We never jump in unsafe conditions.

### How long does the whole thing take?
Budget three to four hours at the dropzone. That covers check-in, the safety briefing, gear fitting, the flight to altitude, the jump itself, and collecting your video and photos afterwards.
