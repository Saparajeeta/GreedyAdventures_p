The plan
Step 1
Arrival and check-in
Check in at the dropzone with your government ID or passport, booking confirmation and completed waivers.
Step 2
Safety briefing and equipment
Meet your tandem instructor for the safety briefing, then have your harness and equipment fitted and checked.
Step 3
Flight to altitude
Board the GippsAero GA8 Airvan with your instructor for the climb to jump altitude.
Step 4
Freefall and parachute descent
Exit attached to your tandem instructor, experience the freefall, then descend under the parachute.
Step 5
Canopy flight and landing
Fly under the parachute with your instructor and land back at the Mount Abu dropzone.

Before your jump
• Minimum age 12; ages 12–17 need guardian consent. Bring government ID/passport, confirmation and waivers. Wear sportswear and closed shoes. Follow briefing instructions; arrive in good health and avoid alcohol.

Good to know
Do I need previous experience?
No. Tandem beginners receive a briefing and jump attached to an instructor.
How long should I allow?
Allow a few hours for check-in, briefing, flight and the jump; timing depends on operations.
What are the weight limits and surcharges?
Maximum 113 kg.
₹3,000 extra for 95–104 kg.
₹6,000 for 105–113 kg.
Can I bring my phone or camera?
Personal devices are prohibited during the jump. Choose the included media option instead.
Can friends or family attend?
Yes, spectators can watch from the viewing area.
Can I eat beforehand?
The operator recommends a light meal.
Is insurance included?
No participant insurance is supplied.
What happens in bad weather?
The tandem page offers weather rescheduling without additional jump charges. Refund eligibility follows the booking terms.
