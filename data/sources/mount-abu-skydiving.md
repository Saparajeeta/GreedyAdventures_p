# Tandem Skydive — Mount Abu, Rajasthan
A tandem skydiving jump in India over Mount Abu, Rajasthan.

## Mount Abu packages and pricing
Package options for the Mount Abu tandem skydive:
• Handycam Video (₹29,999 per person excl. GST): Filmed by your instructor. Includes safety briefing and equipment, tandem instructor, freefall, parachute descent and landing, and first-jump certificate.
• Outside Cameraman (₹39,999 per person excl. GST): Filmed by a cameraman alongside you. Includes safety briefing and equipment, tandem instructor, freefall, parachute descent and landing, and first-jump certificate.
Prices exclude GST. Ask us to confirm your total, including any applicable weight charge.

## The plan
Step 1: Arrival and check-in. Check in at the dropzone with your government ID or passport, booking confirmation and completed waivers.
Step 2: Safety briefing and equipment. Meet your tandem instructor for the safety briefing, then have your harness and equipment fitted and checked.
Step 3: Flight to altitude. Board the GippsAero GA8 Airvan with your instructor for the climb to jump altitude.
Step 4: Freefall and parachute descent. Exit attached to your tandem instructor, experience the freefall, then descend under the parachute.
Step 5: Canopy flight and landing. Fly under the parachute with your instructor and land back at the Mount Abu dropzone.

## Location & Dropzone
Dropzone: Manpur Airstrip, Akra Bhatta, Abu Road, Rajasthan 307026.
Operated by: Skydive India.
Aircraft: GippsAero GA8 Airvan.

## Booking & Requirements
Booking: ₹2,000 deposit; balance on jump day.
Age limits: Minimum age 12; ages 12–17 need guardian consent. Bring government ID/passport, confirmation and waivers. Wear sportswear and closed shoes. Follow briefing instructions; arrive in good health and avoid alcohol.
Personal devices: Personal devices and GoPros are prohibited during the jump. Choose the included media option instead.

## Weight limits and surcharges
Maximum weight: 113 kg.
Surcharges:
• 95–104 kg: ₹3,000 extra per jumper.
• 105–113 kg: ₹6,000 extra per jumper.

## Good to know
Do I need previous experience? No. Tandem beginners receive a briefing and jump attached to an instructor.
How long should I allow? Allow a few hours for check-in, briefing, flight and the jump; timing depends on operations.
Can friends or family attend? Yes, spectators can watch from the viewing area.
Can I eat beforehand? The operator recommends a light meal.
Is insurance included? No participant insurance is supplied.
What happens in bad weather? Weather changes are rescheduled without additional jump charges.
