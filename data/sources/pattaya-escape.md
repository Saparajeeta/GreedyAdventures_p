# Adventures in Pattaya Dropzone
Pattaya, Thailand. The busiest dropzone in the region and where most of our jumpers start. Loads run every day of the year, the exit is straight out over the Gulf of Thailand, and the landing area is a two-minute walk from the packing shed.

## Highlights of Pattaya Dropzone
• Loads every day of the year.
• Exit over the Gulf of Thailand.
• English, Hindi and Thai instructors.
• Spend about 3 hours at the dropzone.

## Sea View Skydiving — Tandem Jump over Pattaya
The straightforward first jump. Same aircraft, same instructors and the same sea view — you exit at 13,000 feet, freefall for around sixty seconds and fly the coastline under canopy. No cameras, no extras: the cheapest way to find out what freefall actually feels like.

## FAQs about Pattaya Dropzone
### When is the best time to visit Pattaya Dropzone?
Year round. Loads run every day of the year.

### Is the jump itself any different from the more expensive packages?
No. Same aircraft, same altitude (13,000 ft), same instructors, same sixty seconds freefall. The difference is only whether it is filmed.

### Can I add video later?
Yes — you can add video and photos at the dropzone on the day, if a camera slot is free.

### Can I use my own GoPro?
No. Dropzones do not allow unlicensed jumpers to carry cameras, for safety reasons.

### What should I wear?
Trainers and comfortable clothes. No sandals, no loose jewellery.
