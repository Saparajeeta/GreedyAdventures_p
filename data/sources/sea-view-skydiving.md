# Sea View Skydiving — Tandem Jump over Pattaya
A tandem skydiving experience over Pattaya with ocean views. Guests receive a safety briefing, fly to jump altitude, complete the tandem jump with a certified instructor, and choose from ECO, Deluxe, or VIP package options depending on the media coverage they want.

## Packages and pricing
Shared Inclusions across all packages:
+ Tandem skydive experience in Pattaya, Thailand
+ Safety briefing and tandem instructor support
+ Aircraft ascent and ocean-view jump experience
+ Certificate of completion

Package Options:
• ECO (฿9,500 per person): The jump, nothing else. Includes tandem jump, safety briefing and instructor, aircraft ascent, and certificate of completion. No camera.
• DELUXE (฿12,500 per person): Video and photos included. Includes tandem jump, safety briefing and instructor, aircraft ascent, certificate of completion, plus wrist-mounted Handycam 4K video and high-res digital photos.
• VIP (฿14,000 per person): Personal cameraman and edited film. Includes tandem jump, safety briefing and instructor, aircraft ascent, certificate of completion, plus a dedicated camera flyer jumping alongside you with edited film and photos.

## The plan
Step 1: Arrival and check-in. Sign in at the dropzone and bring your photo ID.
Step 2: Safety briefing and gear fitting. Meet your instructor for the safety briefing and harness fitting.
Step 3: Boarding and the climb. Board the aircraft and climb to jump altitude.
Step 4: Exit and freefall. Exit with your instructor for freefall over the Gulf of Thailand.
Step 5: Canopy flight and landing. Fly under the parachute with your instructor and land back at the dropzone.

## Jump stats and details
• Exit altitude: 13,000 ft with the Gulf of Thailand on the horizon.
• Freefall: around sixty seconds (~60s).
• Canopy flight: five to seven minutes (5–7 min) along the coast.
• Time at dropzone: about 3 hours.
• No experience needed: your USPA-certified instructor flies the jump.
• Same aircraft, altitude and instructors in every package.
